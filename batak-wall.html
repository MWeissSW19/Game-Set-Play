What is the Batak Wall?
The Batak Wall is a reaction and hand-eye coordination training tool used by tennis players. Lights flash one at a time across the wall — your job is to tap each one as fast as you can. The quicker your reactions, the higher your score.
👆
How to play
A coloured circle will light up somewhere on the screen. Tap it — and the next one will appear instantly in a new position. Keep tapping as many as you can before the timer runs out. Only one target is active at a time, so stay focused!
🏆
Choose your challenge
Pick your difficulty level before you play. Beginner gives you more time to react. Club Player speeds things up. Champion is for the fastest reflexes — targets vanish quickly and the grid goes dark between hits.
